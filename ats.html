Which hiring software do companies use?

We read the public job feeds of 34,235 company career sites with at least one open job, across 10 applicant tracking systems (ATS), on 17 November 2026. Together they had 804,602 open jobs. Workday, iCIMS and other large enterprise systems are not included, so this shows startups and mid-size companies.
